About

Hey, Vivian here

I'm a Product Designer with over a decade of experience designing products that matter to people in real ways. The kind of products where getting it wrong could mean someone loses money, motivation, or momentum.

I started my career as a designer and educator in parallel. I've always been drawn to the challenge of making hard things feel clear. That instinct took me to Duolingo in its early days (a team of ~30, before the world knew what would hit 'em), where I spent nearly 8 years building features for 500M+ users across a deeply global, multilingual product.

Then I joined Remitly to take on very different challenges, like learning about what international KYC means and creating a brand new "scrappy" product aimed at supporting small and micro business entrepreneurs make international payments with less complexity. I have been working inside a highly regulated fintech environment, taking this from a stealth web experiment to a core company OKR — $408M in processed volume and a 19-point lift in new-customer conversion while still in "scrappy-mode". Now we're starting to build dedicated features and expanding to more countries.

I specialize in products where the friction is real and the users are often underserved. Compliance flows that need to feel human. Global experiences designed for people whose first language isn't English and whose tech and financial infrastructure isn't the same as yours. I bring particular care to localization — I'm fluent in English, Brazilian Portuguese, and Swedish, have lived across the US, Brazil, and Sweden, and spent years at Duolingo designing for learners in dozens of languages.

Currently I'm informally leading a pod of 4 designers at Remitly, influencing hiring, design direction, and the occasional exec readout. I volunteer with the Scandinavian Society of Pittsburgh, and in 2026 I gave a talk on startups to MBA students at Carnegie Mellon — then came back to judge their pitch competition.

If you're building something worth building, I'd love to hear about it.
